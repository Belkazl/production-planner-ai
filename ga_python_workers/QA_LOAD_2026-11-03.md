# LiftOrbit QA - larger synthetic workloads (2026-11-03)

## Scope and outcome
Private-only QA worker `liftorbit-full-ga-isolation-qa-20261103` (no public domain), running the restored Python GA with `PP_PY_GA_MAX_PROCS=10`. Separate Railway Bun runner built from `ga_python_workers/qa_heavy/run.mjs`.

The runner emitted `LIFTORBIT_HEAVY_ALL_PASS` (2026-11-03, Railway deployment `d83f780d-089f-4e11-9a6e-d64748a70c3b`).

| Scenario | Submitted | Completed | Model size | GA iterations / population | Elapsed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Medium | 10 | 10 | 20 orders per account | 35 / 25 | 1.733 s |
| Larger | 10 | 10 | 36 orders per account | 40 / 25 | 2.519 s |
| Single large | 1 | 1 | 80 orders | 30 / 20 | 2.881 s |

**21 of 21** computations completed and validated. Order identity/account isolation, material delivery timing and worker zero active slots at the end were checked. Peak active Python processes observed: **10**. Test duration: 7.187 seconds total for all three scenarios.

These are simple synthetic models with one equipment item/one operator, a material-delivery constraint, and setup transitions. This does NOT establish performance on complex multi-machine production schedules, high GA iteration limits (up to 15000), or sustained concurrent load. Railway CPU/memory metrics have coarse sampling and are not a substitute for per-process memory profiling.

## Mandatory production release gates
1. **Durable job state**: `python-ga-upstream.mjs` currently creates `const jobs = new Map()`. A Bun restart or multiple replicas loses local session-to-job mapping. The Python `ProcessJobManager` also holds jobs, queue and results in-process and loses them on worker restart. Store account-scoped job identity/progress/results/resume_state durably and define restart recovery/cancellation/lease semantics. Use a stable authenticated account ID rather than a session cookie hash.
2. **Isolated QA authentication and persistence**: `auth-runtime.js` currently routes auth to `PP_AUTH_GATEWAY_ORIGIN`, which is wired on the QA app to the live auth gateway. Do not create test users against the live DB or write QA results into client storage. Create isolated QA auth storage or a fully synthetic test account backend.
3. **Authenticated end-to-end acceptance**: browser login, load account model, plan, progress, stop, resume, manual locking, persist/reload plan, logout and other-account isolation through the Bun bridge.
4. **Production rollout**: fail-closed mode, staged canary flag, rollback drill, telemetry and alert thresholds, realistic production-scale 100-500 order schedules, 1000-15000 generations, provider/DB checks and key rotation.

Production app and old gateway were **not** switched to the QA worker. This is a QA benchmark, not a release approval.
